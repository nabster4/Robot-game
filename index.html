Halloway Works, your home base: Wren's Workshop (build bots, base systems and new rooms), the Charging Bay (bots recharge here) and Storage (unlimited). Build the Garage (gear), Lab (research, memories) and Command Room (quick travel, recall) to open them up. Around it lies one connected world: the Green Plains to the south, Snowy Plains to the north, Mountains to the east, the Fiery Volcano to the west, the Saltglass Coast to the south-east, and the Sky Islands floating high above the north-east. Power a Warden's beacons to open its dome, in any order — the Sky Islands open last. Caves hide treasure, memory fragments and lost bot parts.

Your hotbar stacks parts and supplies up to 20 per slot. Sell salvage at each biome's shop for Botbucks and buy weapons, gear (jetpack, fire boots), premium bots and upgrades. Bot batteries drain; low bots fly all the way home to recharge and fly back on their own, and you can swap squad bots with ones waiting at home. Watch out for fall damage and your own bomb blasts. Once you enter a boss dome, there is no way out until the boss falls.
