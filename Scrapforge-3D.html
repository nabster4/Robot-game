Robots hang out in camps and leave you alone until you attack them or start an uplink. Climb anything, glide from high places, launch skyward from activated beacons to reach sky islands, and hunt for hidden Scrap Sprites (every 3 grows your stamina).

Each zone hides signal beacons — follow their light pillars. Activate one and hold its uplink ring while the machines counter-attack. Bring every beacon online to open the Core Gate and face the zone's boss. Destroyed robots and hidden salvage caches drop parts — craft them into companion robots and upgrades at the Workshop, any time.
